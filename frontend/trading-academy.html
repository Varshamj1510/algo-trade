IPO CASE STUDY · OCTOBER 2026

Jio Platforms: a big debut, and the valuation question

Use this recent listing to separate a company’s growth story from the price investors pay for its shares.

For learning, not a recommendation
What happened Jio Platforms began trading on 28 October 2026 The Hindu reported a premium at the market debut.
Headline valuation US$137 billion Reuters reported this valuation and ranked Jio India’s second-most valuable firm on 28 October.
The question to investigate Was the issue price justified? A popular brand and strong demand do not, on their own, prove a share is fairly priced.

How to analyze the IPO

  1. Understand the business: identify how Jio earns revenue and what could drive future growth.
  2. Check the valuation: compare the offer price with earnings, cash flow, debt, and listed peers—not just the company’s name or size.
  3. Separate debut from fundamentals: a listing premium reflects initial trading demand; it does not guarantee future returns.
  4. Review the risks: test whether growth, pricing, investment needs, and competition could disappoint the expectations built into the price.

Try the return calculation

Once you verify the issue price and listing price from a reliable source, calculate the listing-day move:

((listing price − issue price) ÷ issue price) × 100

Then ask: would you still buy at the listing price, and what evidence supports your answer?
