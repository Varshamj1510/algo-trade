How practice signals work

The app checks each catalog stock’s daily price change. It does not use current quotes or technical indicators.

  • BUYDaily change is +1% or more.
  • SELLDaily change is −1% or less.
  • HOLDDaily change is greater than −1% and less than +1%.

BUY/SELL target and stop levels are set 2% from the price. Confidence is a heuristic score, not a probability.
