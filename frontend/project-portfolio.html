Platform modules

Built around the complete market workflow.

Each module solves a separate task but shares the same account, market data, user scope and interface conventions.

Market intelligence

Market overview, stocks, indices, gainers, losers, sectors, watchlists and historical chart exploration.

Technical analysis

RSI, MACD, EMA, SMA, VWAP, Bollinger Bands, ATR, candlesticks and volume-based analysis.

Strategy engine

User-defined AND/OR conditions, built-in strategies, clear BUY/SELL/HOLD outcomes and historical reviews.

Portfolio and orders

Holdings, account cash, P&L, trade history, journals, market/limit orders and open-position workflows.

Research and backtesting

Historical moving-average backtests, cost assumptions, drawdown, win rate, alpha research and held-out evaluation.

Risk and alerts

Position sizing, stop-loss planning, risk/reward calculations, price alerts and browser notifications.

System architecture

From browser interaction to persistent account state.

The application uses a single-origin Flask architecture with separate services for market data, strategies, backtesting, trading and external integrations.
